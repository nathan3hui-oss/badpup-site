隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 11 日

本政策只适用于 iOS 游戏「逆子 · Bad Pup」。

保存在手机上的内容

你的关卡进度、骨头、狗狗、配饰和设置都保存在你的设备上；如果你开启了 iCloud，也会同步到你自己的 iCloud（Apple 的 iCloud 键值存储）。我们没有服务器，看不到这些内容。

Game Center

如果你登录了 Game Center，成就由 Apple 按照 Apple 的隐私政策处理。

挑战码

「挑战」里分享给朋友的挑战码只是一段文字，由你自己复制或通过系统分享面板发出，不会经过我们的服务器。

广告

游戏里的广告由 Google AdMob 提供：大多是你自己选择观看的奖励视频（比如骨头翻倍、复活），偶尔在过关后会出现一次全屏广告。AdMob 可能会收集设备信息（如设备标识、IP 地址、广告互动和诊断数据）来展示和统计广告，详见 Google 隐私政策。

在法律要求的地区（欧洲经济区、英国、瑞士），游戏会先征求你的同意，你可以随时在游戏的「设置 → 广告隐私选项」里修改。游戏不会请求读取你设备的广告标识符（IDFA），广告不会跨应用追踪你。

儿童

游戏适合所有年龄，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

本政策的变更

如果本政策有变更，我们会更新这个页面和上面的日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy covers the iOS game “逆子 · Bad Pup” only.

What stays on your phone

Your level progress, bones, dogs, accessories and settings are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account (Apple’s iCloud key-value storage). We have no server and cannot see them.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple handles your achievements under Apple’s privacy policy.

Challenge codes

A challenge code you send a friend is plain text that you copy or share through the iOS share sheet yourself. It never goes through a server of ours.

Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob: mostly rewarded videos you choose to watch (for example to double your bones or get another go), and now and then one full-screen ad after a cleared level. AdMob may collect device information (such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics) to show and measure ads, as described in Google’s privacy policy.

Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent first; you can change your choice any time in Settings → 广告隐私选项 (Ad privacy choices). The game does not ask for your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), and ads do not track you across other apps.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
